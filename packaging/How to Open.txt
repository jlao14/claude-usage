CLAUDE USAGE - HOW TO OPEN

Claude Usage is a small menu bar app. It shows how much of your Claude
plan you've used and what your Claude Code sessions would cost.
It's an unofficial app, not made by Anthropic.

YOU NEED
- macOS 14 Sonoma or later
- Claude Code, signed in with your Claude account (for the plan limits)

INSTALL
1. Drag "Claude Usage" onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open Applications and double-click Claude Usage.

FIRST LAUNCH
Apple hasn't reviewed this app, so macOS blocks it the first time.
1. When macOS says it can't verify the developer, click Done.
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to the Claude Usage
   message, then confirm.
4. When asked about the Keychain, click "Always Allow". This lets the
   app read the login Claude Code already saved.
5. Allow notifications if you want alerts at 50%, 75% and 90% usage.

A gauge icon then appears in the menu bar. Click it to see your usage.
Open Settings from that menu to change alerts and the menu bar display.

PRIVACY
- Your Claude login is sent only to api.anthropic.com, to read your usage.
- Claude Code's local logs are read on your Mac and never uploaded.
- No analytics, no tracking.

UNINSTALL
Quit it from its menu, then drag Claude Usage from Applications to the Trash.
